ACME Industrial Corp - Annual Report Excerpt (Fiscal Year 2025)

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis

Total revenue for fiscal year 2025 was $4,820 million, an increase of 8.4% compared to
$4,447 million in fiscal year 2024. The increase was primarily driven by growth in the
industrial automation segment, which grew 14.2% year over year, partially offset by a
3.1% decline in the legacy components segment.

Gross margin was 41.3% of revenue, up from 39.8% in the prior year, reflecting favorable
input cost trends and pricing actions taken in Q2.

Operating income was $612 million, representing an operating margin of 12.7%, compared to
$531 million and 11.9% in fiscal year 2024.

Net income attributable to shareholders was $438 million, or $3.12 per diluted share,
compared to $379 million, or $2.69 per diluted share, in the prior year.

Total debt outstanding at year end was $1,340 million, down from $1,510 million, as the
Company repaid $170 million of its 2026 senior notes. The debt-to-EBITDA ratio improved
to 1.6x from 2.0x.

Cash flow from operations was $705 million, compared to $612 million in the prior year.
Capital expenditures were $198 million. Free cash flow was $507 million, up 21.6%.

The Company repurchased $150 million of common stock during the year and paid dividends
of $112 million.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our results depend on continued demand for industrial automation equipment, which may be
affected by macroeconomic conditions, interest rates, and customer capital expenditure
cycles. A prolonged downturn in industrial capital spending could materially and adversely
affect our revenue and margins.
