ACME Industrial Corp - Q1 Fiscal Year 2026 Earnings Release

Revenue for the first quarter was $1,265 million, up 6.1% year over year, slightly below
analyst consensus of $1,290 million.

Gross margin was 40.6%, down 70 basis points sequentially due to input cost inflation in
raw steel and semiconductors.

The Company reported earnings per diluted share of $0.74, compared to $0.71 in the prior
year quarter, and reiterated full-year guidance of $3.20-$3.35 per diluted share.

Management noted order backlog declined 4% quarter over quarter, citing softer demand in
the automotive end market, while backlog in data center infrastructure grew 22%.

The Company announced a new $300 million share repurchase authorization and maintained its
quarterly dividend of $0.30 per share.
