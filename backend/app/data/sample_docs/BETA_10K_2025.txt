Beta Consumer Holdings - Annual Report Excerpt (Fiscal Year 2025)

Net sales decreased 2.3% to $2,910 million, compared to $2,979 million in the prior year,
reflecting softer discretionary spending and a 5% decline in unit volumes, partially offset
by pricing gains of 2.7%.

Gross margin contracted to 34.1% from 36.0%, primarily due to elevated promotional activity
and freight costs.

Operating income declined to $214 million from $301 million, an operating margin of 7.4%
versus 10.1% in the prior year.

The Company's total debt increased to $980 million from $760 million following the
acquisition of a regional distribution network, raising the debt-to-EBITDA ratio to 3.4x
from 2.1x.

Free cash flow was $96 million, down from $205 million, reflecting lower operating income
and higher working capital needs.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

The Company operates in a highly competitive and promotional retail environment. Continued
margin pressure, elevated leverage, or a further slowdown in consumer discretionary spending
could impair our ability to service debt and fund planned capital investments.
